# Marketing

SEO, paid media and content production.

> **All four entries here share one author** (AgriciDaniel). They're consistent with each other by construction, but that's concentration risk — a single maintainer's availability, and a single point of view on what good practice looks like. Worth knowing before you build a workflow on them.

## Organic search

- **[claude-seo](https://github.com/AgricIDaniel/claude-seo)** `[Skill]` (13.5k★) — universal SEO suite for Claude Code: 25 sub-skills and 18 sub-agents.
  - *Covers:* technical SEO, E-E-A-T, schema, GEO/AEO, backlinks, local SEO, maps intelligence, semantic clustering, e-commerce, international, Google APIs, and PDF/Excel reporting.
  - *Optional extensions:* DataForSEO, Firecrawl, Banana.

- **[codex-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/codex-seo)** `[Skill]` (554★) — the Codex-first equivalent: 26 workflows, 24 TOML agents, deterministic reports.
  - *Use when:* you're on Codex rather than Claude Code. Same territory as above — pick the one matching your agent, not both.
  - *Integrations:* DataForSEO, Gemini, Google, Firecrawl. GEO/AEO, Core Web Vitals, schema, backlinks, local and maps.

## Paid media

- **[claude-ads](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-ads)** `[Skill]` (7.9k★) — paid-media operations across 12 ad platforms: Google, Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat, X.
  - *Does:* source-grounded audits, deterministic scoring, versioned JSON reports, and capability-gated account changes.
  - *Note:* unvetted. The capability gating on account changes is the part to verify yourself before pointing it at live spend.

## Content

- **[claude-blog](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-blog)** `[Skill]` — blog suite: 30 sub-skills, 5 agents, a 5-gate delivery contract, dual-optimized for Google rankings and AI citations.
  - *Use when:* producing search-facing written content at volume.
  - *Note:* unvetted.
