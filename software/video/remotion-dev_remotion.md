## Notes

### 2026-08-06, from research

- Videos from React components, driven by a frame number.
- Use when: you want the biggest ecosystem (templates, a player, rendering on AWS Lambda, most agent video pipelines) or your team already thinks in React.
- The license is the catch: free for individuals and small teams, but a for-profit company past a small headcount needs a paid company license. Check remotion.pro before company work. Every tool rendering through Remotion carries it too.
- Node, plus Chrome or Chromium to render. `npx create-video@latest`
